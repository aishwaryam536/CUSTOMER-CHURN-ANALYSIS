# Customer Churn & Revenue Analysis – Insights

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn, revenue, monthly charges, customer demographics, contract types, payment methods, internet services, and geographic patterns using Power BI.

The analysis is presented through two interactive dashboards:

- Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard
- Customer Revenue & Charges Analysis Dashboard

---

## 2. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard reports the following key metrics:

- **Total Customers:** 9,799
- **Churn Customers:** Approximately 3K
- **Churn Rate:** 27.64%
- **Total Revenue:** 59.11M
- **Average Monthly Charges:** 163.06
- **Average Tenure:** 36.87

These KPIs provide an overall view of the customer base, churn level, revenue, and customer charging patterns.

---

## 3. Customer Churn Analysis

### Churn by Contract Type

The dashboard compares churn across different contract categories, including One Year, Two Year, Monthly, and Month-to-Month contracts.

The visual allows comparison of churn levels between contract types and can help identify contract categories that require closer attention for customer retention.

### Churn by Subscription Type

Churn is compared across Premium, Basic, Standard, Family, and other subscription categories.

This helps identify differences in customer attrition across subscription plans.

### Churn by Payment Method

The dashboard provides a comparison of churn across payment methods such as:

- Credit Card
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Cash
- E-Wallet
- Bank Transfer
- Net Banking

The distribution allows payment methods to be compared as part of the overall churn analysis.

### Churn by Internet Service

Customer churn is analyzed across different internet service categories, including:

- DSL
- Cable
- Fiber Optic
- Fiber
- 5G
- No Internet Service

This provides visibility into churn patterns across different service types.

### Churn by Senior Citizen

The dashboard compares churn between senior-citizen and non-senior-citizen customers.

The visualization shows a substantially larger customer count in the non-senior-citizen category.

---

## 4. Revenue Analysis

### Revenue by State

Revenue varies across states.

The dashboard shows higher revenue values for states such as Delhi, Gujarat, Karnataka, and Maharashtra compared with several other states.

This geographic comparison can help identify regions contributing strongly to overall revenue.

### Revenue and Monthly Charges

The dashboard reports:

- **Total Revenue:** 59.11M
- **Average Monthly Charges:** 163.06

These metrics provide an overview of the financial contribution and charging pattern of the customer base.

---

## 5. Monthly Charges Analysis

### Monthly Charges by Internet Service

Monthly charges are compared across internet service categories.

DSL and Cable show relatively high total monthly charges in the dashboard, while other service categories contribute different amounts.

### Monthly Charges by City

The dashboard compares monthly charges across cities.

Cities such as Bengaluru and Delhi show approximately 142K in monthly charges, followed by cities including Mumbai, Mysuru, Pune, Kochi, Chennai, and others.

This allows geographic differences in customer charges to be identified.

### Monthly Charges by Gender

Monthly charges are compared across customer gender categories.

The visualization provides a comparison of the contribution of different gender groups to total monthly charges.

---

## 6. Contract Analysis

The dashboard compares total charges across contract types.

The displayed values include approximately:

- **One Year:** 19.49M
- **Two Year:** 17.77M
- **Monthly:** 11.09M
- **Month-to-Month:** 10.24M

This comparison shows how total charges vary across different contract categories.

---

## 7. Customer Demographic Analysis

### Revenue by Age

The dashboard compares total revenue across age categories.

The visualization shows differences in revenue contribution between the displayed age groups, allowing customer revenue to be examined from a demographic perspective.

### Dependents by City

Dependents are also compared across selected cities.

The visualization shows differences in the number of dependents across locations.

---

## 8. Business Value

The analysis provides a consolidated view of customer churn and financial performance.

The dashboard can help businesses:

- Monitor customer churn
- Compare churn across customer segments
- Analyze revenue by location and demographics
- Compare charges across internet services
- Understand contract-level revenue contribution
- Identify customer segments requiring further investigation
- Support data-driven customer retention strategies

---

## 9. Conclusion

The Customer Churn & Revenue Analysis project demonstrates how Power BI can transform customer-level data into interactive business intelligence dashboards.

By combining churn analysis with revenue and customer-charge analysis, the project provides a broader view of customer behavior and financial performance.
